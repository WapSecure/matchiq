## Owner decisions, in priority order

1. **Branch for submission (R9).** Link `tree/dev`, or promote to `main` first.
2. **Hosting (R10, S9, S10).** Deploy the approved VPS option before the freeze, or submit without a hosted demo and rely on the video plus local instructions.
3. **Record and upload the video (R5–R7).**
4. **Category (R16) and team roster (R17).**
5. **Deck format (S6).**
6. **Licence (S2).** Keep MIT, or decide on a commercial licence later. A change after submission does not revoke rights already granted under MIT for the submitted commit.
