# MatchIQ enterprise readiness review

Reviewed 23 October 2026 against product specification sections 38–58, engineering policies, ADRs and frontend source. This is a source review, not production certification, a penetration test or a completed customer study. No live cloud integration or SLA was verified.

## Recommendation

Position MatchIQ as a football editorial workspace that makes insights traceable and gives producers control over publication. Focus first on broadcaster and digital publisher production teams. A broad promise to serve broadcasters, clubs, developers and consumers at once will dilute the first commercial release.

The strongest foundations are the deterministic facts engine, evidence references, reproducible replay, editorial interventions, provider disclosure and tenant-aware identity design. A polished interface helps procurement confidence; commercial readiness also requires licensed data, proven operations and a buyer who will pay for the workflow.

## Market benchmark

Opta Live advertises live research, historical context, graphics and expert support: https://www.statsperform.com/products/opta-live/. Sportradar describes a real-time insights dashboard and API: https://sportradar.com/media-tech/data-content/sports-insights/. These are vendor descriptions, not independently verified performance claims.

Inference: AI commentary and live statistics alone are weak differentiation. Test whether evidence inspection, fast editorial decisions, reproducible replay and regional language quality materially improve a producer's work. Do not compete on the breadth of established data archives before obtaining rights and validating demand.

## Visual acceptance still required

This pass changes source presentation, not the whole interaction design. Inspect light and dark themes at desktop, tablet and mobile widths; long club/player names, translated strings, empty/error/loading states, dense cue lists, 200% zoom and reduced motion. Confirm preview label fits at narrow widths. MQ-041 applied the selector migration and validated the public and protected workflows. Landing screenshots were inspected on desktop light and mobile dark. Inspection exposed a preview minimum-width clipping issue missed by the existing overflow test; the hero now uses one explicit mobile grid column and the preview can shrink. MQ-041 added preview-bounds regression coverage. MQ-041 subsequently captured all six screens in both themes at desktop/mobile widths with producer sign-in. Long-content and translated-interface states still require qualification.

Next design iteration should use a producer-observed task sequence: choose match → assess freshness → inspect insight → review evidence → control publication → inspect audit result. Prefer clear hierarchy, compact tables and meaningful status over additional ornamental cards. Retain the established semantic colors until contrast and real layouts justify changes.

## Copy rules

Use concrete actions and short sentences. Distinguish live data, replay, static sample, simulated fault, template and model output. Avoid unsupported superlatives, decorative prose dashes, invented ROI, universal language coverage and badges that imply unearned certification. Keep meaningful hyphens such as single sign-on and time-weighted, and mathematical ranges and score separators. Typography is not evidence of authorship.
